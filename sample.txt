# TechCorp Employee Handbook & Engineering Guidelines (2026)

## 1. Company Overview
TechCorp is a leading technology company specializing in artificial intelligence and autonomous developer tooling. Our flagship product is Project Antigravity, an advanced agentic AI coding assistant developed in partnership with Google DeepMind.

## 2. Work Policy & Office Hours
- Standard business hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST, Monday through Friday.
- Remote Work: Employees are permitted up to 3 remote working days per week upon manager approval.
- Core Collaboration Hours: All team members must be online and reachable between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM EST.

## 3. Financial Benefits & Allowances
- Learning & Development: Full-time engineers receive an annual education stipend of $2,500 for books, courses, and certifications.
- Wellness Reimbursement: Employees can claim up to $75 per month for gym memberships or fitness activities.
- Home Office Setup: New hires receive a one-time equipment grant of $1,000 for ergonomic desks, chairs, and monitors.

## 5. Security & Data Protection Rules
- All production code must be reviewed by at least two senior engineers before merging.
- API keys, credentials, and customer data must never be committed to Git repositories.
- Production database access requires multi-factor authentication (MFA) and temporary token generation.
